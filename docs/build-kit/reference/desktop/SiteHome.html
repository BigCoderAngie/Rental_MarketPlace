Got questions?

Short answers about renting, listing and the booking fee.

Do I pay anything to rent?

No. Sending a request is free and renters never pay RentLoop. You pay the rent and deposit to the owner when you collect.

Why can't I see the owner's name or number?

To keep everyone safe. Names, numbers and addresses unlock for both of you once the owner accepts your request.

What if several people want the same item?

The owner sees every request and picks one. The others are told the dates are taken, and their details are never shown.

How does RentLoop make money?

Owners pay a small booking fee only when they accept a renter. We only earn when you earn.

What happens with the deposit?

You pay it to the owner at collection and get it back when you return the item as you got it.

Can my hire business use RentLoop?

Yes. Add your business name and logo, invite staff, send WhatsApp quotes and keep checkout records.
