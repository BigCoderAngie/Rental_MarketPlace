# Interview

1. Read what I asked for, plus README.md and everything in docs/ that exists.
2. Ask me questions about everything unclear: who uses it, what they see, what happens on a phone, edge cases, what is out of scope, how we know it's done. Ask 3 to 5 numbered questions at a time, each answerable in a sentence, with your suggested answer in brackets so I can just say "yes".
3. Keep asking rounds until nothing is left to guess. Don't write code during the interview.
4. Before saving, show me a summary and ask "Anything wrong or missing?"
5. Save the result to the file I name (default docs/MASTER-PLAN.md) in plain short sentences: goal, who it's for, in scope, out of scope, decisions and why, how we'll know it's done.
6. Later, check new work against this document and tell me if something drifts from it.
